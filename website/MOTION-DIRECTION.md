## Motion thesis
Focal: vertical campaign lanes with opposing speeds, tile cutouts entering as a set at first load (700ms), unaltered photography. Pause button persists in the wall; hover/focus pauses transiently in addition to persistent control. Hidden/offscreen wall pauses via IntersectionObserver/page visibility; explicit pause preference persists per tab. Reduced-motion defaults to a static art-directed wall and disables spatial transitions.
Continuity: category filters fade between states without hiding all content by default; service images crossfade when their associated task changes. Gallery has image detail and next/previous buttons, Escape and focus return.
Feedback: controls use short color/arrow responses; form gives draft-ready state with explicit next link. No repeated section fly-ins. A light display-word entrance is the only load choreography beyond the wall.
Budget: no animation library, no video/WebGL dependency, transform-only continuously moving lanes. Avoid rAF scroll loops, expensive full-screen filters, layout thrash and opaque loaders. Visible HTML without JS; images responsive WebP/AVIF, below-fold lazy.
